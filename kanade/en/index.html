See the notes.

Notes stream down toward the keyboard, so you can tell what every part is playing before you hear it.

See the drums, too.

Kick, snare, hi-hat and toms each get their own lane, so the pattern reads straight off the screen.

The FULL view stacks an 88-key keyboard for every part, so you can take in the whole arrangement at a glance.

GS and XG, played properly.

Open a file written for GS or XG and any tone the synth doesn’t have is substituted with the closest one it does. No parts drop out.

Play through the Mac’s standard tones or a SoundFont of your own, and any MIDI hardware you’ve connected over USB or Bluetooth works just as well.
