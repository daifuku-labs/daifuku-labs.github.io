Melody Eraser — iPhone / iPad

Explore how your
favorite songs are made.

An iPhone app that erases the melody so you can hear just the backing — or make a minus-one track. Free to try.

Download on the App StoreLanguages: Japanese, English, Spanish (Latin America), Korean, Portuguese (Brazil), Traditional Chinese

Not just vocals.

Violin, saxophone — a lead played on an instrument comes out too. Listen to what has been going on behind the melody all along.

Load a song, then move the slider.

Import a music file you already have and set how much of the melody to fade, from 0 to 100%, while it plays. There is nothing else to set up.

The stereo stays.

Unlike classic left-minus-right cancellers, the width of the mix survives. Your backing track never collapses to mono.

Hear only what you removed.

Switch to Melody Solo and just the faded side is left. It is there for learning parts and transcribing by ear.

Save it as a file.

Export the result as m4a / ALAC / wav. For practicing your instrument, or for singing along.

Nothing leaves your phone.

Everything runs inside the iPhone. Your music is never sent anywhere.

Start with the free demo track.

Or bring your own song — it works right away.

Download on the App Store

Works with your own DRM-free audio files (m4a / mp3 / wav / ALAC / AIFF / FLAC, stereo). Songs purchased from the iTunes Store work as-is. Songs saved to your device from music subscription apps are DRM-protected and cannot be used.
